## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Homepage highlights
The homepage SHALL summarize and link out to every other core section (About & FIRST, Team Leadership, Our Robots, Outreach & Impact, Sponsors, Join/Contact) and SHALL surface live highlights from the Blue Alliance widget and Instagram feed capabilities.

#### Scenario: Homepage links to every core section
- **WHEN** a visitor loads the homepage
- **THEN** they can find a visible link or teaser card for About & FIRST, Team Leadership, Our Robots, Outreach & Impact, Sponsors, and Join/Contact Us

#### Scenario: Homepage shows current robot
- **WHEN** a visitor loads the homepage
- **THEN** the most recent entry from the Our Robots collection (name + photo) is featured prominently, e.g. in the hero or a dedicated section

#### Scenario: Homepage embeds live feeds
- **WHEN** a visitor loads the homepage
- **THEN** the Instagram grid (social-media-feed) and a Blue Alliance highlights teaser (blue-alliance-widget) are both present

### Requirement: About Us & FIRST page
The site SHALL provide a page explaining the team's mission/history and introducing what FIRST Robotics Competition is, for visitors unfamiliar with the program.

#### Scenario: Visitor learns what FIRST is
- **WHEN** a visitor loads the About Us & FIRST page
- **THEN** they see a plain-language explanation of the FIRST Robotics Competition program alongside the team's own mission and history

### Requirement: Outreach & Impact page
The site SHALL provide a page summarizing the team's local community work and in-school demos.

#### Scenario: Visitor reviews outreach activity
- **WHEN** a visitor loads the Outreach & Impact page
- **THEN** they see a short summary of the team's community work and school demos, editable via the site's CMS without a code change
